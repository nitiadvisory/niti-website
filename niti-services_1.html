Why Niti

Strategy &
Guidance.

Niti Advisory Services is a boutique compliance and regulatory advisory firm founded by former regulators. Derived from Sanskrit, Niti signifies strategy, guidance and sound governance—principles that form the cornerstone of our approach to serving clients.

We believe compliance is more than a regulatory requirement — it is an integral part of effective governance and risk management. Adopting a risk-based approach, we focus on the areas that matter most, helping clients implement controls that are proportionate to their risk profile and business strategy.
