About
Niti.

Niti Advisory Services is a boutique compliance and regulatory advisory firm founded by former regulators. Derived from Sanskrit, Niti signifies strategy, guidance and sound governance—principles that form the cornerstone of our approach to serving clients.

Our founders bring extensive regulatory and industry experience gained at the Dubai Financial Services Authority (DFSA), where they supervised a wide range of leading global and regional financial institutions. Their expertise spans diverse business models, including fund and asset management, private banking, brokerage, financial advisory, arranging and investment banking.
Combining deep regulatory insight with practical industry experience, Niti helps clients navigate complex regulatory environments with confidence—meeting regulatory expectations while advancing their strategic business objectives.

Our Work.

From licensing applications to ongoing compliance monitoring — we deliver bespoke regulatory solutions across DIFC and ADGM for funds, private banks, brokerages and advisors.
